Featured Products

📈 AI Product / Product Builder

Portfolio Pulse

Problem: Investors often have portfolio updates, market signals, and company information spread across different places, making it difficult to quickly understand what needs their attention.

I designed and built Portfolio Pulse, an AI-powered portfolio intelligence prototype that helps investors bring company updates, market signals, and internal context into one place. The experience includes portfolio monitoring, company views, an attention queue, market signals, forecasts, and reporting.

Product takeaway: Building Portfolio Pulse helped me think about the full decision-making workflow: what information matters, how it should be prioritized, and how AI can help surface useful signals without overwhelming the user.

🚨 Featured AI Product

Operational Intelligence Platform

Problem: Engineering teams often need to detect operational issues quickly and investigate incidents before customer impact grows.

I designed and built an end-to-end operational intelligence prototype that enables engineering teams to upload telemetry, detect anomalies, and receive AI-assisted investigation guidance. The solution combines a React frontend, FastAPI backend, Isolation Forest anomaly detection, and LLM-powered operational insights to demonstrate how machine learning can become a practical product.

Product takeaway: I designed the experience around operator workflows, trustworthy outputs, stable APIs, and explainable AI so engineers can confidently investigate operational incidents.

These projects demonstrate my background in machine learning, deep learning, and applied AI. While they are technical implementations rather than end-user products, they shaped how I think about explainability, trustworthy AI, and building products powered by machine learning.

🩺 AI / Healthcare

Multi-Modal Survival Prediction for Skin Cancer

Problem: Healthcare prediction is only useful when AI is understandable, not just accurate.

I developed survival prediction models using clinical, genomic, and imaging data, combining survival analysis, deep learning, and SHAP explainability to improve prediction transparency.

Technical takeaway: This project strengthened my understanding of multimodal learning, explainable AI, and the importance of building models that clinicians can understand and trust.

🌍 NLP / Crisis Intelligence

Disaster Tweet Classification

Problem: During emergencies, critical information is often hidden within large volumes of noisy social media data.
